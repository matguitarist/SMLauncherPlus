- Back up a profile's saves with ⌘B, and restore one from the new Saves sheet (⇧⌘B) or from a zip. Your saves are backed up before each restore, so you can go back.
- Every update backs up your saves first, and doesn't start if it can't.
- Rename, duplicate, show in Finder and delete profiles on the new Profiles page or in the Profile menu. Deleting can keep the saves.
- LCD Grid draws its grid again, and CRT Soft, Sharp and Warm Composite show their full effect with the enhanced renderer.
