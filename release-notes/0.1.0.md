The first release of SM Launcher Plus: install, update and play SuperMetroidRecomp from your own Super Metroid ROM.

- Choose your ROM (.sfc, .smc, or one inside a .zip or .7z). It is checked and read where it is, never copied.
- Download the latest SuperMetroidRecomp, checked against its SHA256SUMS, and play straight to the title screen.
- Update to a new release in one click, keeping your saves, settings and mods, and roll back if it gives you trouble.
- Turn the game's mods and their options on or off, install .snesmod packages, and choose the files a mod asks for, like the Zero Mission map art.
- Set display, filters, widescreen, sound and controls in Settings, and move your data to another drive.
- When the game stops with an error, read its log and save a report without your ROM.
